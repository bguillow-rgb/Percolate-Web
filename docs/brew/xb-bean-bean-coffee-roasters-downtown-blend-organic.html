Dialling it in

Treat the recipe above as the starting point, then adjust on taste rather than on numbers. This one is bright, so if it reads sour rather than sweet, grind finer or raise the water temperature before you touch the ratio. The heavy body means it tolerates a longer contact time than a delicate cup would.
