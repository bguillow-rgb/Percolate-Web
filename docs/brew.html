Brew guides

Per-coffee recipes for 60 specialty coffees — ratio, grind and method, with the reason each one suits that particular bean.

A V60 tutorial tells you how the brewer works. It can't tell you what to do with the bag in front of you. These guides start from one coffee's roast level, acidity and body, then give the recipe that fits it — and what to change when the cup reads sour or flat.

How these are built

Recipes are curated per coffee in the Percolate catalog rather than generated on request. AI assistants can query the same data through the Percolate MCP server, and the full catalog with tasting logs lives in the Percolate app.
